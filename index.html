Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about our electric scooters, charging, and support.

Our Ampere electric scooters offer excellent range for daily commuting. For instance, the Ampere Nexus delivers up to 136 km on a single charge, while the Magnus EX offers a certified range of 100+ km. Real-world range may vary slightly based on riding conditions and payload.
Most of our electric scooters feature fast and efficient charging. Typically, it takes around 3 to 5 hours to charge the battery from 0% to 100% using the standard home charger provided. You can easily plug it in at your home or office just like a smartphone.
High-speed models like the Ampere Nexus and Magnus EX require a valid two-wheeler driving license and RTO registration. However, low-speed models (top speed capped at 25 km/h) do not require a license or registration to ride, making them perfect for teenagers and short commutes.
We provide a standard 3-year warranty on both the vehicle and the battery. Additionally, you can opt for an extended warranty package that covers your battery and essential components for up to 5 years, giving you complete peace of mind.
